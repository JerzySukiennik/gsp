# Audio sources

All recordings are real files, only trimmed, looped and mixed — nothing is synthesised.

- `engine_loop.wav`, `ignition.mp3` — NASA, Space Shuttle launch natural sound (STS-131, STS-135), public domain. Originals in `src/`, from nasa.gov/audio-and-ringtones.
- `explosion_*.ogg`, `decouple.ogg`, `thud.ogg`, `chute.ogg`, `space.ogg`, `click.ogg` — Kenney (kenney.nl) Sci-Fi Sounds and Impact Sounds, CC0.
